About M/s M.S. Construction

M/s M.S. Construction is a premier civil engineering and infrastructure contracting enterprise led by Anurag Tiwari. Headquartered in Medininagar, Palamu, Jharkhand, we execute high-profile civil infrastructure projects spanning national highways, expressway pavements, railway viaducts, heavy-axle track infrastructure, and structural frameworks across India.

Operating on the foundational ethos of BUILD • PLAN • GROW, our multidisciplinary teams of licensed civil engineers, project managers, and certified technicians operate state-of-the-art machinery to deliver projects adhering to strict national guidelines including IRC:37, IRC:58, MoRTH compliance, and IS 456 monolithic formwork.

OUR VISION

To be India's foremost and trusted civil infrastructure enterprise, recognized for setting benchmarks in engineering precision, durable highway and railway viaduct networks, and nation-building infrastructure with zero compromise on safety.

OUR MISSION

To engineer, execute, and deliver critical transportation corridors, heavy-axle rail viaducts, and monolithic structural complexes through innovative technologies, stringent IRC & MoRTH compliance, and punctual project completion.

Leadership & Vision

Anurag Tiwari

Managing Director & Infrastructure Leader

Leading M/s M.S. Construction with engineering precision and nationwide execution. Under his leadership, the enterprise executes vital civil infrastructure, highways, railway viaducts, and large-scale structural works across India.

With headquarters in Medininagar, Palamu (Jharkhand) and projects spanning national corridors, Anurag Tiwari has championed strict technical compliance including IRC:37, IRC:58, MoRTH guidelines, and heavy axle rail viaduct standards.

The firm specializes in flexible & rigid highway pavements, elevated pier casting, monolithic Mivan shuttering, and prestigious government EPC tenders.
