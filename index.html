About Us

Enterprise Civil Infrastructure

Executing India's Highway, Rail Viaduct & Structural Corridors

M/s M.S. Construction is a premier civil infrastructure contracting enterprise led by Anurag Tiwari. Headquartered in Medininagar, Palamu (Jharkhand), we execute complex highways, railway viaducts, monolithic structural frameworks, and prestigious government EPC projects across India.

Our core capabilities span flexible & rigid highway pavements, elevated pier casting, girder launching, ballastless track infrastructure, and commercial structural works. Every project adheres strictly to national engineering benchmarks including IRC:37, IRC:58, MoRTH guidelines, and heavy-axle rail standards (25t – 32.5t).

With a steadfast commitment to BUILD • PLAN • GROW, our experienced engineers combine advanced mechanized formwork (Mivan Shuttering, IS 456) with meticulous quality control and on-site safety standards.

OUR PROCESS

How We Execute Construction Projects

A systematic, engineering-driven lifecycle from site feasibility to final commissioning.

STEP 01

Site Feasibility & Survey

Conduct topographical survey, drone LiDAR mapping, and initial feasibility studies to define precise corridor boundaries.

STEP 02

DPR & Cost Estimation

Formulate Detailed Project Reports (DPR), comprehensive Bill of Quantities (BOQ), and transparent rate analysis per CPWD/MoRTH.

STEP 03

Contract & Approvals

Formalize milestone agreements, secure government & municipal clearances, and align statutory regulatory compliances.

STEP 04

Geotechnical & Soil Testing

Perform core drilling, standard penetration testing (SPT), and bearing capacity analysis for rock-solid foundations.

STEP 05

Engineering Design & BIM

Engineer vetted CAD/BIM structural models, seismic load analysis, and execution blueprints conforming to IS & IRC codes.

STEP 06

Railway Viaduct & Tracks

Cast heavy rail viaduct piers, launch segmental box girders, and lay ballastless track beds under Indian Railways & ABSS norms.

STEP 07

Mobilization & Machinery

Deploy heavy civil machinery, batching plants, calibrated testing equipment, and skilled manpower under CPM controls.

STEP 08

Civil Execution & QA/QC

Execute substructure and superstructure civil works under multi-tier quality checks, cube strength tests, and site safety.

STEP 09

Commissioning & Handover

Complete structural load audits, statutory compliance certification, punch-list clearance, and formal milestone handover.
